# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release. Two runs a day, at 06:00 and at 12:00, read the calendar for
today and tomorrow, work out the counterpart of each meeting, and leave a one
page briefing in the plugin's own folder before the meeting starts. Internal
meetings, personal entries and blocks of focus time are recorded as skipped
rather than researched, and every claim that came off the web carries its
source. The page lists both days in time order with a Prepare now button, and
keeps the last twenty briefings searchable by name.
