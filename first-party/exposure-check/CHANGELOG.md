# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release. A password is hashed in the page and checked against the Pwned
Passwords corpus by sending five characters of the hash and matching the other
35 locally, with a padded reply so the size of the answer gives nothing away.
The services a business depends on are checked against the public breach list by
domain, worst first, with the categories of data that came out named in full.
An address is checked only with a key of your own, and the two keyless halves
keep working when there is none.
